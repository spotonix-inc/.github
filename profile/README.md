# Spotonix

**AI can answer your business questions. Spotonix gives you answers you can defend.**

Spotonix is your AI analyst. It asks when a business term is unclear, shows the plan before it queries your data, and reuses what your team clarifies.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Choose Meaning** | When a business term is unclear, Spotonix asks. Pick one of the suggested meanings or write your own for this analysis. |
| **Review the Plan** | Inspect the selected definitions and planned calculation before execution. |
| **Reuse Meaning** | What your team clarifies in an answered question is available to later questions, so you stop teaching the same meaning twice. |
